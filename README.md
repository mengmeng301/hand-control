## ✋ What Is hand-control?

hand-control is an end-user application for controlling a laptop with hand gestures using only the webcam. You do not need a programming background, an extra controller, or special hardware. You open the app, keep the camera window visible, and make gestures in front of the webcam.

The camera window is your guide. It shows your camera view and draws a hand skeleton on your hand. If your hand is inside the frame and the skeleton appears, the app can see your hand. If the skeleton does not appear, move your hand into the center of the camera view and try again.

## 🖐️ Gesture Controls

| Gesture | What it does |
|---|---|
| 👍 Thumbs up | Volume up (keeps going while you hold it) |
| 👎 Thumbs down | Volume down (keeps going while you hold it) |
| 👉 Point right with your index finger | Right arrow |
| 👈 Point left with your index finger | Left arrow |
| 🤏 Pinch thumb and index together | Space bar (one press per pinch) |

Hold thumbs up or thumbs down when you want the volume to keep changing. Release the gesture to stop. For arrow keys, point clearly to the right or left with your index finger. For the space bar, pinch your thumb and index finger together once. One pinch sends one space bar press.

## 👀 The Camera Window

The camera window is the most important part of the app. It shows a live picture from your webcam with a hand skeleton drawn on top of your hand. That skeleton is how you know the app is tracking you.

When the skeleton follows your fingers, gestures will work. When the skeleton is missing or jumps around, the app is having trouble seeing your hand. Move your hand closer to the camera, keep it inside the frame, and add a little more light in the room.

Leave the camera window open while you use gestures. If you close it, the app stops listening for gestures.

## 💡 Tips for Reliable Gestures

- Keep your hand roughly an arm's length from the camera, fully inside the frame.
- Use the camera window to check that the skeleton is drawn on your hand before you make a gesture.
- Face a window or a lamp so the front of your hand is lit, rather than lit from behind.
- Make one gesture at a time and pause briefly between gestures.
- Hold thumbs up or thumbs down steadily; the volume keeps moving while the gesture is held.
- Point with a straight index finger and the rest of your hand relaxed for left and right.
- Pinch clearly and separate your fingers between pinches, so each pinch counts as one press.
- Close other apps that also use the webcam, such as video call software, if the camera window stays black.

## ❓ Common Questions

**Do I need to install anything else?** No. The app runs from its folder. Extract the zip, open the folder, and start the application.

**Does it work without a webcam?** No. The webcam is how the app sees your gestures.

**Can I use it while the camera window is hidden?** Keep the camera window open. It shows what the app sees and keeps gesture tracking running.

**Why does the volume keep changing after I lower my hand?** The volume continues while the gesture is held. Hold it for less time, or move your hand out of the camera view to stop.

**Does the app record or send my camera video anywhere?** The camera view is shown in the window so you can see your hand skeleton. Nothing else is needed from you to use the app.

**What if my antivirus blocks the file?** This is part of the same first-launch warning. Choose to keep the file and run it, following the security warning steps above.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

**The camera window is black.** Close other apps that use the webcam, then start hand-control again. Check that no camera cover or privacy shutter is closed.

**No hand skeleton appears.** Move your hand into the middle of the frame and improve the lighting. A plain background behind your hand helps.

**The wrong action fires.** Make gestures more slowly and hold each one for a moment. Keep your other fingers relaxed so the app does not confuse a point with a pinch.

**The app will not open.** Check that you extracted the zip first, then open the application from inside the extracted `HandControl` folder rather than from the zip preview window.

**Volume does nothing.** Click once on the window or app you want to control before making gestures, so the volume keys go to the right place.
